## Not downloadable here (paywalled - use your institutional access)

- Chelikowsky group, *Machine learning assisted search for Fe-Co-C ternary compounds with high magnetic anisotropy*, APL Mach. Learn. 2, 046103 (2024). CGCNN screen of ~1e6 substitutional structures - the closest published analogue to this workflow's `decorate` lane.
- Mehl et al., *The AFLOW Library of Crystallographic Prototypes*, Parts 1-4, Comput. Mater. Sci. (2017-2024). The 1,783-prototype reference itself.
- Nieves et al., Comput. Mater. Sci. 168, 188 (2019) - published Novamag paper (the arXiv preprint above is the open version).
